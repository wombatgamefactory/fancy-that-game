FANCY THAT! - RULES SUMMARY
============================

GOAL
----
Collect patisserie reward cards by building colour patterns on your personal
board, then score points by multiplying the ingredient symbols on your cards
by the matching ingredient tiles you've collected.

COMPONENTS
----------
- Tiles: 100 tiles (5 colours × 5 ingredient symbols)
- Reward Cards: 50 unique cards (10 per ingredient)
- Personal Boards: One 5×5 grid per player
- Market Board: One shared 6×6 grid
- Cupcakes: 5 per player at game start

SETUP
-----
1. Deal 16 cards face-down to form the game deck
2. Reveal the top 4 cards face-up as the card market
3. Fill the 6×6 market board with tiles from the bag
4. Each player takes a personal board and clears space for their scoring pile
5. Randomly determine first player

YOUR TURN (5 STEPS)
-------------------

STEP 1 — SWEEP
Choose a row or column on the market board and declare either a colour or an
ingredient symbol. Take all tiles matching your declaration.

Bonus: If you clear a row/column completely, take 1 extra tile from anywhere
on the market board.

STEP 2 — PLACE TILES
Place all swept tiles anywhere on your 5×5 board. No adjacency required —
tiles can go in any empty cells. All tiles taken must be placed this turn.

STEP 3 — CLAIM (Optional, maximum 1 per turn)
If tiles on your board form the colour pattern on any face-up card in the card
market (in any rotation or reflection), you may claim it.

When claiming:
1. Remove exactly 1 tile from those that formed the matching pattern
2. Place it in your personal scoring pile
3. Tuck the card face-down under your board
4. Draw the top card from the game deck to refill the card market

Blocked Spaces: The cell where you removed the tile becomes permanently blocked
and cannot accept new tiles.

STEP 3.5 — MOVE TILES (Optional, using cupcakes)
You may spend a cupcake to move one tile from its current cell to any other
empty cell on your board. You can do this at any point during your turn. Each
move costs 1 cupcake.

STEP 4 — REFILL MARKET
If 6 or fewer tiles remain on the market board and the bag has tiles, refill
the market to restore empty spaces.

CUPCAKES
--------
You start the game with exactly 5 cupcakes. They are a limited resource that
can be spent to move tiles on your board (1 cupcake per move). Cupcakes cannot
be earned during play. At game end, each unspent cupcake is worth 1 point.

CARD PATTERNS
-------------
All patterns fit within a 2×2 ghost area on your board.

Tier 1 — Single (1 tile, 0 symbols)
Any 1 tile of the stated colour anywhere on the board.

Tier 2 — Domino (2 tiles, 1 symbol)
Two orthogonally adjacent tiles of the stated colours. Valid horizontally or
vertically.

Tier 3 — L-tromino (3 tiles, 2 symbols)
Three tiles in an L-shape within a 2×2 area (one corner cell empty). Four
valid rotations.

Tier 4 — 2×2 square (4 tiles, 3 symbols)
All four cells of a 2×2 area filled with the stated colours.

SCORING
-------
At game end, calculate your score as follows:

1. For each ingredient:
   (symbols on your claimed cards) × (tiles in your scoring pile) = ingredient score

2. Sum all five ingredients for your base score

3. Add 1 point for each remaining cupcake

Final Score = Base Score + Cupcakes

Example: You hold cards with 4 Almond symbols and your scoring pile has
3 Almond tiles. Almond score: 4 × 3 = 12 points.

GAME ENDS WHEN
--------------
1. Card Market Exhausted (Normal End)
   The game ends when the last card is claimed from the card market. The current
   round is completed so all players have had equal turns, then scoring occurs.

2. Board Overflow (Immediate End)
   If a player sweeps tiles and cannot place all of them on their 5×5 board
   (insufficient empty cells), the game ends immediately. All other players get
   exactly one more turn each, then final scoring occurs. The player who triggered
   overflow does not get additional turns.

3. Market Tiles Exhausted
   If all tiles are removed from the market board and the tile bag is empty,
   the game ends immediately. No further sweeps are possible. Final scoring
   occurs.

TIEBREAKER
----------
If players are tied on points, the player with the most reward cards claimed
wins. If still tied, share the victory.

KEY STRATEGY
------------
The central strategic tension: Sweeping by colour controls which colours you
collect (for pattern building) but randomises which ingredient symbols you get.
Sweeping by symbol controls your end-game multiplier potential but randomises
the colours you receive.

Players manage two collections simultaneously:
- Tiles on their board (for future patterns)
- Tiles in their scoring pile (for end-game multipliers)

These objectives frequently conflict, which is the strategic heart of the game.

Cupcakes add another layer: limited movement flexibility that lets you optimize
your board layout, but spending them reduces your end-game score.
